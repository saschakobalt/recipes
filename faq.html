Frequently Asked Questions

What defines a "classic" recipe here? expand_more

All recipes on this site focus on authentic, traditional preparation methods. I aim to preserve original culinary heritage rather than modified modern shortcuts.

Can I substitute certain ingredients? expand_more

While you can replace ingredients based on local availability, sticking to the listed original items ensures the authentic taste and texture intended for the dish.

Are the yield and calories exact? expand_more

Nutrition information and serving sizes are estimated standard averages per portion to give you a helpful baseline for meal planning.

How often are new recipes added? expand_more

New classic recipes are tested, documented, and published regularly. Follow along on Instagram for updates.

Can I share or reuse these recipes? expand_more

Feel free to share links to the recipes. If you re-post photos or content on social media or blogs, please provide direct credit and a link back to BY ALÉ.

Who made this website and why? expand_more

This website was created by Sascha(@saschakobalt), special thanks to Eric(@erichastedt). The goal is to share traditional recipes and preserve culinary heritage. The website is currently under development(aka beta). No AI was used and will never be used.
